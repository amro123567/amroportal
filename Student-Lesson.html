A circle has a radius of 6 centimeters. What is the length, in centimeters, of an arc with a central angle of π/3 radians?

Correct. Arc length s = r·θ = 6 · π/3 = 2π cm. This skill now counts toward your Geometry & Trigonometry mastery.
